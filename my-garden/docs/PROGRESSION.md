# Progression — Canonical Rules

**Source of study data:** only `my-garden`’s own log.  
**Not connected** to the CGPSC Mains Tracker app.

Daily target: **6 hours**.

---

## 1. Tree — 4 continuous days

```text
Day N     ≥ 6 hours
Day N+1   ≥ 6 hours
Day N+2   ≥ 6 hours
Day N+3   ≥ 6 hours
        ↓
   ONE permanent tree
```

- Days must be **consecutive** (streak of goal days).
- Missing a day **breaks** the current 4-day count (already-planted trees stay).
- Each completed 4-day block earns **one** new tree (or seed to plant).
- Tree **never** disappears.

Species (when implemented): random from Oak, Pine, Cherry, Banyan, Maple, Jacaranda, Neem, Sakura, etc.

---

## 2. Cherry blossom — 6 continuous days

```text
6 continuous days of ≥ 6 hours each
        ↓
   Cherry blossoms on the tree
```

- Same continuous streak idea, extended to **6** days.
- Blossoms appear on the tree tied to that streak (typically the newest / matching tree).
- Miss a day → streak resets for blossom progress; existing blossoms on permanent trees can stay (presentation choice: keep beauty on past trees).

---

## 3. Fruits — good marks

```text
You log a test score that counts as "good marks"
        ↓
   Fruits on the related tree
```

Suggested tiers (simple, adjustable later):

| Score | Fruit |
|------:|-------|
| ≥ 60% | Apples (good) |
| ≥ 70% | Peaches |
| ≥ 80% | Mangoes |
| ≥ 90% | Golden fruits |

- Score is entered **in my-garden** (manual test log), not imported from the mains app.
- Higher marks → richer fruit look.

---

## What breaks / what stays

| Event | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Miss a day (&lt; 6h) | Current continuous streak resets |
| Tree already planted | **Stays forever** |
| Blossoms / fruits on old trees | Remain as memory of past work |
| Long break | Can dull leaves later (optional polish) — never delete trees |

---

## Explicit non-goals for v1

- No link to mains tracker hours / scheduled tests / subjects
- No XP, coins, check-in XP, battle pass
- No farming rewards without real 6h days
