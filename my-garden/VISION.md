# Project Vision: **The Discipline World**

*A persistent open-world game that grows from real-life study logged inside **my-garden**.*

---

# Independence

**This project does not receive data from the CGPSC Mains Tracker app.**

No shared API, no reading the mains SQLite DB, no Godot/garden_api feed.

Study hours and test scores for the world are recorded **only in my-garden**.

---

# Core Philosophy

This is **not a gamified study app.**

This is **a living world** where every object exists because of real work you logged for this garden.

There are **no artificial rewards** (coins, gems, XP grinding, battle pass, loot boxes, etc.).

The only way to progress is by actually studying.

Years later, this world becomes a visual archive of my preparation journey.

---

# Core Progression (canonical)

These three rules are the product:

| Continuous study | World |
|------------------|--------|
| **4 days** in a row, each **≥ 6 hours** | **One permanent tree** |
| **6 days** in a row, each **≥ 6 hours** | **Cherry blossoms** |
| **Good marks** (test score you log here) | **Fruits** on the tree |

Everything else in this vision (weather, regions, wildlife, cabin) is optional atmosphere built **later** on top of these rules.

**Location:** this folder (`my-garden`) — study log + Unity world, self-contained.

---

# What the world does

* Own study log (hours + optional scores)
* Progression: 4-day tree · 6-day blossom · fruits from marks
* Rendering the world
* Growing environment
* Exploration, weather, animations, sound, immersion (over time)

Example signals (from **my-garden** log only):

```
Today's Study     6 Hours
Current Streak    6 Days   → cherry blossom
Last test         82%      → fruits
Trees earned      from each block of 4 continuous 6h days
```

# Progression System

## Trees (core rule)

Every **4 continuous days** of **≥ 6 hours** study (logged in my-garden)

↓

A permanent tree is planted.

The tree NEVER disappears.

It remains forever as proof of discipline.

Different species are chosen randomly:

* Oak
* Pine
* Cherry Blossom
* Banyan
* Maple
* Jacaranda
* Neem
* Sakura
* etc.

This makes every forest unique.

---

## Leaves

Daily completion affects health.

Completed day → Healthy green leaves

Missed study → Leaves become dull.

Long break → Leaves fall.

Tree survives.

When routine resumes → New leaves slowly grow.

No punishment.

Only neglect and recovery.

---

## Cherry blossoms (core rule)

**6 continuous days** of **≥ 6 hours** each

↓

**Cherry blossoms** on the tree.

---

## Fruits (core rule)

**Good marks** (test score logged in my-garden) decorate the tree.

| Score | Fruit |
|------:|-------|
| 60%+  | Apples |
| 70%+  | Peaches |
| 80%+  | Mangoes |
| 90%+  | Golden fruits |

Higher marks make the forest visibly richer.

| Phase | Season |
|-------|--------|

# Subjects Become Regions

Each subject has its own biome.

| Subject | Biome |
|---------|-------|

| Economy | Fruit orchard |

---

# Ancient Structures

Subjects begin unfinished.

Examples: broken bridge, ruined temple, destroyed observatory, collapsed library.

As study progresses they slowly rebuild.

No upgrade button. Construction simply happens over time.

---

# Camp

Player owns a cabin.

Contains: bookshelves, maps, study trophies, achievements, fireplace, furniture, decorations.

New rooms unlock through long-term study.

---

# Legendary Trees

Major milestones create monumental trees.

| Milestone | Tree |
|-----------|------|
| Completed Paper I | Massive Banyan |
| Paper II | Sacred Oak |
| Interview | Legendary Tree |

Visible from anywhere in the world.

---

# Memory Stones

Every weekly test creates a permanent stone.

Contains: week number, study hours, score, date, optional memory.

Example: *"This was the week I finally understood Inflation."*

Years later, walking through the world becomes walking through personal history.

---

# Delayed Growth

The world does not update immediately.

Finish studying → Log hours in my-garden → Sleep → Next day open the world → A cinematic plays.

Seeds sprout. Flowers bloom. Bridge repairs. Birds return.

The world grew while you were away.

This makes progression feel magical.

---

# No Instant Rewards

Instead of rewards appearing automatically, the player participates.

Study complete → Receive seed → Walk to empty patch → Plant seed → Water once → Leave → Return tomorrow → Tree has grown.

The study earned the reward. The interaction builds attachment.

---

# NPCs

The world contains peaceful NPCs.

Example: Old Librarian who acknowledges learning.

* "You've mastered Mughal Administration."
* "Today's revision strengthened your memory."

No quests. No grinding. Only recognition.

---

# Time

World has a real day-night cycle.

Morning birdsong · Afternoon sunlight · Evening golden hour · Night moonlight, fireflies, owls, stars.

Ambient sounds encourage simply walking and reflecting.

---

# Camera

The game opens directly into the world.

No dashboards. No statistics. Just the player.

The camera should feel cinematic: slow pans, wind, leaves moving, water reflections, particles.

Everything should feel alive.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Imagine opening the game after several years.

Instead of seeing statistics, you walk through hundreds of trees, cherry blossoms, fruit orchards, bridges, ancient temples, waterfalls, birds, foxes, deer, campfires, mountains, libraries, your cabin.

Every object represents real effort.

The world is not just a game.

# Guiding Principles

* Real life drives all progression.
* Nothing can be purchased.
* Nothing can be farmed inside the game.
* Every visual change must correspond to genuine study effort.
* The world is peaceful, reflective, and personal—not competitive.
* The game is a **living journal**, not a productivity dashboard.
* Years of discipline should naturally create a world that tells the story of the person who built it.
